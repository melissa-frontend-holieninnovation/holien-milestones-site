Weekly capacity and AU intake

Four team slots each week. Reserve one for bugs and use a second when urgency and operational impact justify it. Protect at least one V2 UX slot and one feature slot. In a one-bug week the fourth slot goes to the oldest ready UX improvement or feature request of comparable priority.

AU intake stays open every week through 31 March. Record submission date, affected operation, urgency, impact, workaround, dependencies and the next realistic review or delivery window. A critical production issue can interrupt immediately. Record the work displaced, its revised date and the milestone impact for Yi and Ben. For comparable work, finish the scheduled V2 slice and take ready requests in submission order. If an older request is blocked, retain its place and record what will unblock it.
