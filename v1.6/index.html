Expansion scope and March targets

The seven later native modules and dispatcher guided practice have dated rows above. Full native migration and production guidance depend on approval and additional capacity. For the six broader ideas, use the following provisional windows if separately staffed by 18 December.

  • 4 Jan – 12 Mar · Nonessential theme variants and expanded guided tutorials. Validate accessible variants and first-use journeys on the approved screens.
  • 4 Jan – 19 Mar · Invoice scanning and advanced driver self-booking. Agree document handling and booking rules in January, then pilot each flow.
  • 4 Jan – 24 Mar · Routing intelligence/ML and broader reporting. Agree data access, scope and evaluation criteria in January. Review routing results and reconcile reporting outputs before the release decision.

At the December review, record an owner and acceptance criteria for each approved addition. Any item outside the funded scope gets an explicit deferral at the March closeout.
